摘  要

针对专利计量研究中“分析方案依赖人工设计、自动化系统规划能力不足”的问题，本文提出一种数据感知驱动的多智能体专利分析框架。现有 LLM 系统在代码执行与报告生成方面已具备一定能力，但在方案设计阶段普遍缺少对真实数据分布与结构特征的前置感知，导致任务容易模板化、结论针对性不足。为解决该问题，本文构建了由 Strategist、Methodologist、CodingAgent、Reviewer 组成的四智能体协作系统，并通过 LangGraph 实现端到端流程编排。

在方法上，本文提出三项关键机制。第一，数据感知前置机制：在规划前引入 DataPreview 与 GraphPreview，提取列级统计、跨列关系、图结构特征和分层抽样语义样本，并驱动 LLM 生成可检验研究洞察。第二，双知识图谱协同机制：将因果图谱用于约束研究假设空间，将方法图谱用于约束分析执行空间，实现“理论解释”与“方法可执行”的协同。第三，两轮迭代优化机制：以第一轮执行结果为反馈，触发第二轮差距修补与验证性分析，形成“探索—验证”闭环。

实验采用四方案渐进式消融设计（D_baseline0、A_template、B_data_aware、C_iterative），在三个典型专利计量问题上完成 12 组对比实验。结果表明，完整方案 C_iterative 在自动化指标与 LLM-as-Judge 评分上均达到最优，平均总分为 24.00/25；相比 A_template，B_data_aware 在针对性与创新性方面表现明显提升。实验同时揭示“知识图谱约束悖论”：知识约束在缺乏数据证据协同时可能导致性能下降。

本文工作从“执行自动化”进一步推进到“规划自动化”，为专利计量分析提供了可复现、可扩展、可追踪的系统化实现路径，对技术情报分析和知识产权决策具有实际应用价值。

关键词：多智能体系统；专利计量；数据感知；知识图谱；自动化分析

Abstract

This thesis addresses a core limitation in patent analytics automation: existing LLM-based systems are increasingly capable at execution but still weak at analysis planning. In many cases, plans are generated without sufficient grounding in dataset-specific evidence, resulting in template-like tasks and limited specificity. To solve this problem, we design a data-aware multi-agent framework for patent analytics, including Strategist, Methodologist, CodingAgent, and Reviewer, orchestrated by a LangGraph workflow.

The proposed method contains three key components. First, a pre-planning data-awareness mechanism introduces DataPreview and GraphPreview to extract column-level statistics, cross-column relations, graph-structural signals, and stratified semantic samples, then converts them into testable insights through LLM reasoning. Second, a dual-knowledge-graph collaboration mechanism is designed: a causal graph constrains hypothesis space, while a method graph constrains executable analysis space. Third, a two-round iterative mechanism refines plans using first-round execution feedback, enabling an exploration-to-validation loop.

We evaluate the framework with progressive ablation across four modes (D_baseline0, A_template, B_data_aware, C_iterative) on three representative patent bibliometrics tasks (12 runs in total). Results show that the full mode C_iterative achieves the best performance in both automated metrics and LLM-as-Judge evaluation, with an average score of 24.00/25. Compared with A_template, B_data_aware substantially improves specificity and innovation. We also observe a knowledge-constraint paradox: graph constraints alone may degrade performance when not coupled with data evidence.

The study advances automation from execution-level intelligence to planning-level intelligence, and provides a reproducible, extensible, and traceable framework for patent analytics in real-world scenarios.

Keywords: multi-agent system; patent bibliometrics; data awareness; knowledge graph; automated analytics
